# FixIt AI — Diagnose it. Fix it.

FixIt AI is a mobile web app that helps people in Nigeria diagnose and fix problems with generators, water pumps, cars and household appliances through a guided chat with an AI technician. Every AI call runs on Gemma 4.

Built for Hack Day LASU Epe (Hacktoberfest 2026, powered by MLH).

## What it does

- **Diagnose:** describe a problem in English or Pidgin. FixIt asks one question at a time and ranks the likely causes live.
- **Guide:** for jobs you already know, like changing a tyre or priming a pump, FixIt asks about your situation first, then gives step-by-step instructions with safety warnings inside each step.
- **See:** upload a photo of a machine, nameplate or dashboard warning light, and FixIt uses what it sees.
- **Stay safe:** risk is judged by the job (DIY, Careful, Pro or Emergency). Pro jobs and emergencies end in a Safety Stop that lists what you can safely do now, with a Call 112 button for emergencies.
- **Get help:** send a ready-made report to a technician on WhatsApp.

## How it uses Gemma 4

| Job | Model | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conversation, diagnosis, risk judgement, step-by-step guidance, Pidgin | gemma-4-31b-it | gemma-4-26b-a4b-it |
| Reading photos | gemma-4-26b-a4b-it | gemma-4-31b-it |

Both models are called through the Gemini API (Google AI Studio) from server-side edge functions, so API keys never reach the browser. Photos are analysed and not stored. There is no login.

## Why open-source AI

Gemma 4 is released under the Apache 2.0 licence. That lets FixIt stay free for users, opens the door to an offline version on phones using the small Gemma 4 models, and makes it possible to fine-tune on Nigerian repair knowledge.
